## Variant: Calm Editorial

### Design stance
The trip is a story worth telling. Chrome disappears; content leads. Feels like a well-designed travel journal, not a database.

### Key choices
- **Layout:** single quiet hero carousel, one accent section ("Coming up"), collapsible past trips
- **Typography:** Georgia/Iowan serif for display, system sans for UI, letterspaced small-caps labels
- **Color:** paper tones (#F7F4EF), ink (#2B2722), single forest-green accent
- **Interaction:** dot carousel, collapsible past-trip list, hover states

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: emotional pull, brand distinctiveness, photo-forward trips
- Weak at: dense data, power users scanning many trips

### Best for
Content-forward audiences who want Wanderaid to feel like a memory-maker, not a tool. Low screen density tolerance.
