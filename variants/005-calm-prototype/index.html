Home · user lens

Answers “what’s going on across my trips?” with live ticking countdown, action-needed calls, grouped feed, and personal money.

  • Hero is tappable instead of adding a noisy CTA row.
  • Countdown is the emotional serif moment.
  • Feed lines are grouped by actor + action.

Trip Overview

The per-trip front door: cover photo, countdown, basics, who's going, invite code, group chat, activity, and trip sections.

  • Uses the trip name as the main page title.
  • Cover image gives each trip its own feel.
  • Unread dots keep chat/vote calls visible without badges everywhere.

Places

POI shortlist with tags, pin markers, weighted voting, one locked-in marker linked to the itinerary.

  • Vote buttons update the visible score in-place.
  • Weak yes is positive (+0.5), abstain is absent/null.
  • Locked-in is visually separate from pinned.

Itinerary

A day-grouped coordination spine for flights, hotel stays, and prebooked activities with participant avatar chips.

  • Day headers read like editorial sections.
  • Calls can live inside the timeline and surface back to Home.
  • Undated backlog preserves maybe-later tasks.

People

Traveler list; tap a person to open their detail view and see their footprint across places, expenses, bookings, votes, role, timing, and money.

  • Person detail is a cross-cutting derived view.
  • Message button demonstrates DM entry point.
  • Money status stays glanceable before detail.

Money

The fair ledger: user-first balances, quick on-the-go expense add, expense list, and repayment plan.

  • Tap “View repayment plan” to swap from list to settlement guidance.
  • Money uses serif as an emotional number.
  • Quick add stays lightweight and non-formy.

Memories

The guest book for after-trip closure: short memories, external album links, and a link back to chat. No ratings.

  • Warm closure, not review mechanics.
  • External albums are MVP-compatible.
  • Chat tie-in keeps the story thread alive.

Chat

First-release text chat: one trip channel plus user-level direct messages, with minimal unread dots.

  • Shows trip thread and cross-trip DM list on one surface.
  • No typing indicators, read receipts, media, or threads.
  • Entry points exist from Trip Overview and People details.
