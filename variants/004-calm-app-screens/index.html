Current Trip / Trip Detail

Trip detail becomes the focused version of the dashboard hero card: emotional photo anchor, current-user next actions, and compact section entry points.

  • Strong destination identity without becoming a photo-only landing page.
  • Next actions and user balance appear before generic navigation cards.
  • Trip sections become low-noise tappable cards instead of button stacks.

All Trips

A calm archive where current, future, and past trips are scannable without crowding the home dashboard.

  • Segmented grouping mirrors dashboard language.
  • Current trip gets hero treatment; future/past become compact rows.
  • Create trip stays available but secondary.

Expenses / Balances

Money is user-first: “what do I owe or get back?” before raw group totals.

  • Large personal balance card anchors the screen.
  • Expenses, balances, and people stay accessible through calm segmented tabs.
  • Rows emphasize payer, split context, and next settlement action.

People / Participants

Participants should feel collaborative, with account/guest status visible but not heavy-handed.

  • Organizer/owner status is clear.
  • Guest display-name collaboration stays supported.
  • Balances and roles can be glanced without opening each person.

Places

Saved places use editorial cards plus just enough map context to feel travel-native.

  • Map preview is calm and suggestive, not a full GIS interface.
  • Category pills make the shortlist easy to scan.
  • Votes/notes can feed dashboard attention items.

Itinerary / Planning

A timeline-like layout makes daily plans, votes, bookings, and TODOs feel structured without becoming dense.

  • Days act as editorial sections.
  • Action-needed items stand out inside the day.
  • Completed planning items stay present but quieter.

Create / Join / Invite

The profile-menu actions expand into calm forms/sheets with enough context to avoid confusion.

  • Create and join use the same card language.
  • Invite preview confirms the destination before joining.
  • Guest collaboration remains visible as a product principle.

Confirmations

A wider in-app confirmation pattern gives destructive copy room to breathe while preserving strict cancel-first safety.

  • Copy says who is affected and what stays unchanged.
  • Cancel is visually quiet but easy to hit.
  • Destructive action is explicit and separated.
